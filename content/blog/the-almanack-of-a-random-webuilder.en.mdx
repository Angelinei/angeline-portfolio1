## Or how starting into indie hacking

not from a developer point of view and internationalisation in progress (documentation required on the path)

by Angéline Poitout

**Indie Hacking : My “non-technical” journey as a female webuilder** **(apparently that matters, oui oui)**, into the new world of indie hacking.

Indie hacking has completely changed how I approach building online projects. As a non-developer tech tech (wordpress is CMS) diving into this world, I discovered that you don’t need to code from day one to start creating and earning online. This is **the almanack of a random female webuilder** : my personal story of starting into **indie**, not from a developer’s point of view, while progressing with internationalization and keeping detailed documentation along the path.

Whether you’re a complete beginner, a creative professional, or someone who wants to build sustainably without VC pressure, this journey shows that indie hacking is accessible to anyone willing to learn step by step. Practically. And bricks by bricks, right way continue to appear, so let’s continue.

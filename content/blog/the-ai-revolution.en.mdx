For a long time, online private education was better than nothing… but still quite limited. Programs were mostly standardized, feedback came slowly, and many learners felt lost or progressed at a frustrating pace. Misorientation was common, especially when moving into Master’s programs, and challenges like school bullying often went unaddressed in the learning journey.

Then AI changed the game completely.

### Before AI vs After AI

**Before AI**, private online courses followed a one-size-fits-all logic. You had to adapt to the program instead of the other way around. Progress was slow, personalization was minimal, and support was limited to office hours.

**After AI**, we entered the era of **ultra-personalization** and **accelerated learning at scale**. The difference is night and day.

Here are concrete examples of what’s now possible:

- AI that builds a truly custom learning path based on your goals, current level, strengths, and preferred pace.
- Real-time feedback and intelligent tutoring available 24/7.
- Tools that help prevent misorientation by suggesting the right Master’s direction using skills and market data.
- Support systems that detect when a learner is struggling emotionally and offer private, judgment-free help.

This revolution is particularly powerful in the private sector, where innovation moves much faster.

### My 8 micro-products experiment

Each small agent is designed to solve **one specific pain point** and creates a dedicated ecosystem around **existing content** — particularly videos from social media platforms. The philosophy is simple: **one platform = one product**.

Concrete applications include :

. Supportive learning companions that offer emotional support and detect when a student needs extra help.

. **Ultra-personalized learning paths** generated from existing videos on platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or LinkedIn.

. Instant, intelligent feedback on assignments and exercises.

. Applications and learning faster without unecessary videos, because I got this complain a lot from learners when I was into the field for Edtechs in France.

Instead of creating new content from scratch, these AI agents intelligently curate, sequence, and adapt **already existing high-quality videos** into personalized learning journeys. This approach makes learning faster, more relevant, and much more accessible.

### The bigger picture

Private online education powered by AI is moving from “good enough” to truly transformative. By combining ultra-personalization with accelerated learning, we can help learners progress quicker, stay motivated longer, and align their education with real career goals.

This is just the beginning. With small, focused AI agents building ecosystems around existing content, the potential to democratize high-quality, personalized education has never been greater.
